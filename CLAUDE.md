@AGENTS.md

`Working Master Plan.md` holds the open work, in order: read it before starting, follow its
order, and keep it current as decisions change. Durable rules and how the project works are in
`AGENTS.md` (above); the history of finished phases is in `docs/plan-archive.md` (read-only);
dated decisions go in `docs/plan-changelog.md`. When a phase finishes, condense it in the plan
rather than letting the plan grow again.
